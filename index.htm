About

Mark Oliver Spengler is a composer, keyboardist and producer of electronic music. Active since the 1990s, his work blends precise synthesizer programming, detailed sound design and structured electronic composition. His music ranges from chiptune‑influenced pieces to atmospheric soundscapes and emotionally driven studio productions. His releases appear on all major digital platforms.

He was active in the Amiga demoscene during the 1990s under the handles Mindstorm / MSP, and was a member of the groups Apex, Laser Dance, Scoopex and Supercarrier. He contributed to the Sonic Arranger Q&A and composed several demo tracks for the release version. Several early tracker modules are documented in the Amiga Music Preservation archive.
