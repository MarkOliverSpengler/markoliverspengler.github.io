About

Mark Oliver Spengler is a composer, keyboardist and producer of electronic music. Active since the 1990s, his work blends precise synthesizer programming, detailed sound design and structured electronic composition. His music ranges from chiptune‑influenced pieces to atmospheric soundscapes and emotionally driven studio productions. His releases appear on all major digital platforms.

He was a prominent figure in the Amiga demoscene during the 1990s under the pseudonym Mindstorm, contributing to renowned groups such as Apex, Laser Dance, Scoopex, and Supercarrier. He was deeply involved in the Sonic Arranger Q&A and composed several demo tracks specifically for its release version.

His portfolio encompasses studio album releases, commissioned media projects, and collaborative productions. He focuses on modular synthesis, hybrid production techniques, and a meticulous balance between nostalgic textures and modern clarity.
